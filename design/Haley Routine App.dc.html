3eMotion rules
Rules any new motion holds
Transform and opacity only. A colour change is an overlay fading, never an animated colour — the phone is on all day.
steps() over easing for anything pixel. 2–4 steps. Smooth easing belongs to fades and slides, not to blocks.
Nothing over 500ms except the reward chain, which is ~2s and is the only place worth spending time.
Only two things loop forever — the waiting dots and the uncollected-coin idle. Both mean "still true", not "look at me".
Nothing animates on a failure-adjacent state. Sent back, closed for today, wrong PIN, a broken streak: stillness.
Nothing blocks a tap or has to finish before the next screen will accept input.
prefers-reduced-motion — keep the opacity fades, drop every transform and the coin arc. The award still reads: number, counter, XP bar.

Say which of these you want built first, or "cut the milestone stars" / "the check-off is too slow" — every timing above is a number I can change.

4bSound rules + the silent list
Rules
Sine only, peak gain ≤ .22, 20ms attack. No samples, no square waves, nothing percussive.
Haley's actions make sound. Parent actions are silent — PIN entry, approve, send back, close for today, settings. She should never hear the app being administered.
Nothing negative ever sounds. Silence is the design, not an omission.
Nothing runs longer than 1.5s except the alarm.
Auto-quiet in the Nighttime Routine window and after it. The phone is in her room; the alarm chime is the one exception and it stays at its current gain.
One global toggle in parent settings 1l, plus a separate alarm-only toggle so the timer can stay audible with everything else off.

1Haley's Routine & Minecoin App — design system + all screens
Design notes
Navigation: three big bottom tabs — HOME, TIMER, ME (customization). The clock lives in every screen's header; a running timer pins a dark pill above the content on every child screen. The parent area hides behind a small iron-door chip in the Home footer. Theming: pixel bevels + block textures as trim only; content sits on calm parchment. Jersey 25 for headings/numbers/buttons, Nunito Sans for instructions. No red anywhere — "sent back" is calm slate blue. Reward flow: approving banks the coins and shows the handoff screen (1o); the celebration in 1m only plays when Haley taps "Tap to see your coins!" — hers to release, not the parent's. If she isn't there, the uncollected reward waits as a gold button at the top of Home. Tweaks panel: coin balance drives the XP bar; toggle the running-timer pill.

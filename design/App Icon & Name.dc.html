1Routine app — icon, name, manifest rulings
The coin stays. It is the app's own material, already drawn at the right discipline, and it is the cleaner half of the gold/emerald pair. What changes is the ground: the shipping icons put a pale gold coin on pale parchment, and at 48px that is a light smudge on a light square. Ink ground fixes the read and hands the math app the whole light half of the pair. Everything else in the pipeline — 16×16 source, nearest-neighbour integer upscale, opaque plate — already earns its place and is kept.

1cManifest rulings, and what the audit found
1 · BACKGROUND_COLOR — KEEP #f3eee1
This is the splash ground, and its only job is to not flash. The app boots to Home, and Home's body is parchment #f3eee1 under every world theme. The placeholder happens to be correct; it stays.

Note this is deliberately not the icon's ink ground. The splash shows the icon tile floating on parchment, which is exactly what the home screen shows too.
Splash, as installed.
2 · THEME_COLOR — KEEP #f3eee1, AND THEY AGREE
The manifest says #f3eee1 and the index meta says #f3eee1. No contradiction to resolve.

Worth writing down why it must stay neutral: the header strip is user-switchable across five world themes (grass, nether, end, ocean, cherry), and a manifest colour is fixed at install. Any themed value would be wrong for four of the five. Parchment is the one surface every variant shares.

If the two ever diverge, the index meta wins at runtime — Chrome applies the live meta to the status bar, while the manifest value is the install-time default. So they must be kept in sync by hand; the manifest is not the single source.
3 · WHAT THE AUDIT FOUND
Kept, because it already earns its place: the coin as the mark; the nearest-neighbour integer upscale in gen-icons.mjs; the opaque plate; background_color; theme_color; display: standalone, orientation: portrait, relative start_url and scope.

Replaced: the parchment ground, for contrast at 48px. Retired: the 16×16 coin as icon source — the icon now has its own 32×32 master, and the in-app 16px sprite is untouched.

Three findings for the build, none of them artwork:
a. The manifest carries the child's name. APP_NAME, APP_SHORT_NAME and APP_DESCRIPTION are all derived from CHILD_NAME in profile.ts, so the installed identity is "Haley's Routine & Coins" / "Haley". The app's public identity should stop deriving from the child entirely and become app-wide constants. PROFILE_ID must not be touched — it names the database.
b. APP_DESCRIPTION reads "Routines, timers and Minecoin rewards". In-app copy may reference real Minecoins; install metadata is not in-app copy. Suggested: "Do your day, earn gold."
c. Three icon files do not exist: no 192 maskable, no true 180 apple-touch-icon (the index points it at the 192), and no favicon (same). All three are in the export.

The artwork is final and regenerable from master-32.png. The name is the household's call — say which of the five and I will write it into the export's README as the one value, or draw more if none of them is what she would actually say.
